# How to make (Beans)



Ingredients 
- 2Cups of Beans
- Fresh Pepper
- Onion
- Palm Oil
- **Salt**
- Maggi

## Steps

### Steps 1 Per boil the beans
1. Per boil the beans for 10mins
2. Drain the water away 
3. Put fresh water inside the pot

### Steps 2.Cook the beans
1. Pour the washed and per boiled beans inside the pot with fresh water
2. Allow it cook for something 
3. Add your onion
4. Add your pepper 
5. Add your palm oil
6. Cover it and let cook together  for 20mins
